## What is Sentry?

Sentry is a developer-first **error tracking and performance monitoring** platform. It captures unhandled exceptions, traces slow transactions, records session replays, and surfaces the root cause of issues — so you can fix bugs faster and ship with confidence.

Sentry is available as a **cloud-hosted service** at [sentry.io](https://sentry.io) and as a **self-hosted** deployment you run on your own infrastructure.

## How Sentry works

When you install a Sentry SDK in your application, it automatically captures errors and performance data and sends it to Sentry. The platform groups related events, enriches them with context (stack traces, breadcrumbs, user info), and notifies your team through your preferred channels.

```
Your App  ──SDK──▶  Sentry Relay  ──▶  Sentry Platform  ──▶  Your Team
```

1. **SDK** — A lightweight library installed in your app. Sentry SDKs exist for [JavaScript](https://github.com/getsentry/sentry-javascript), [Python](https://github.com/getsentry/sentry-python), [Ruby](https://github.com/getsentry/sentry-ruby), [Go](https://github.com/getsentry/sentry-go), [Java/Kotlin](https://github.com/getsentry/sentry-java), [iOS/macOS](https://github.com/getsentry/sentry-cocoa), [Android](https://github.com/getsentry/sentry-java), [.NET](https://github.com/getsentry/sentry-dotnet), [React Native](https://github.com/getsentry/sentry-react-native), and [more](https://docs.sentry.io/platforms/).
2. **Relay** — An edge proxy that validates, normalizes, and forwards event payloads while protecting your DSN.
3. **Platform** — The core application that processes events, groups issues, evaluates alert rules, and serves the UI.

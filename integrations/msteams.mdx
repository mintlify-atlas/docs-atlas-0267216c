The Microsoft Teams integration connects your Sentry organization to a Teams workspace. It posts alert notifications to channels or users and supports interactive message actions so you can respond to issues without leaving Teams.

## Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Alert notifications" icon="bell">
    Route Sentry alerts to any Teams channel or user based on alert rule conditions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Interactive messages" icon="mouse-pointer-click">
    Assign, ignore, and resolve Sentry issues directly from Teams adaptive card messages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Channel and DM support" icon="message-square">
    Post notifications to team channels or send direct messages to individual users.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Installation

The Teams integration is installed through the Microsoft Teams Marketplace, not directly in Sentry.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Sentry app in Teams Marketplace">
    In Microsoft Teams, open the **Apps** section and search for **Sentry**. Alternatively, use the direct install link from the [Teams Marketplace](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/app/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to your team">
    Click **Add to a team** and select the team where you want to receive Sentry notifications. Click **Set up a bot**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete setup in the General channel">
    After adding the bot, Sentry sends a welcome message to the **General** channel of your team. Click the link in the welcome message to complete the installation in Sentry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize in Sentry">
    You are redirected to Sentry to confirm the connection between your Teams tenant and your Sentry organization. Sign in and click **Accept**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The Teams integration cannot be installed directly from **Settings > Integrations** in Sentry. The installation must start from the Microsoft Teams app store.
</Note>

## Setting up alert notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open alert rules">
    Go to **Alerts > Alert Rules** and create or edit a rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Teams action">
    Under **Then perform these actions**, click **Add action** and select **Send a Microsoft Teams notification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the action">
    Choose the Teams workspace and the target channel or user for the notification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the rule">
    Save the alert rule. Sentry will post to the configured target whenever the rule conditions are met.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Interactive message actions

When Sentry posts an alert to Teams, it sends an adaptive card that includes action buttons:

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| **Resolve** | Marks the Sentry issue as resolved. |
| **Ignore** | Ignores the issue (mutes notifications for it). |
| **Assign** | Opens a menu to assign the issue to a team member. |

These actions update the Sentry issue in real time and reflect the change in the Teams card.

## Linking your identity

To receive personal notifications and use interactive actions, link your Teams identity to your Sentry account:

1. In Teams, open a direct message with the Sentry bot.
2. Send a message or follow the prompt to link your account.
3. Click the link provided to complete the identity connection in Sentry.

## Supported Teams features

- **Team channels**: Post notifications to channels in any team where the Sentry bot has been installed.
- **Direct messages**: Send notifications directly to individual users who have linked their identities.
- **Adaptive cards**: Notifications use Teams adaptive cards for rich formatting and interactive buttons.
